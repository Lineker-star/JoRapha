# Purpose

This pack translates the approved strategic decisions into three baseline documents. They are designed to be reviewed together and refined through stakeholder discovery, clinical governance, legal review and technical validation.

| Document | What it governs | Primary reviewers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PRD v1.0 | Product vision, users, functional scope, offline requirements, release priorities and acceptance. | Sponsor, Product Lead, clinical and UX leads |
| BRS v1.0 | Business objectives, stakeholder needs, operating model, business rules, partner obligations and sustainability. | Sponsor, operations, finance, partners and legal counsel |
| Technical Architecture v1.0 | System components, data model, offline sync, security, interoperability, integrations and deployment. | Solution architect, engineering, security, privacy and clinical leads |

# Recommended approval sequence

1.  Review the BRS with prospective providers, facilities, community representatives and institutional stakeholders.
## 2. Conduct legal and clinical governance review before confirming regulated workflows.
## 3. Approve the PRD scope and MVP boundaries.
4.  Validate offline architecture with a working proof-of-concept on target Android devices and unstable networks.
## 5. Approve architecture decisions, hosting and data protection controls.
## 6. Create detailed user stories, RTM, API contracts, ERD and UX prototypes.
## 7. Only then authorize full implementation and pilot procurement.
